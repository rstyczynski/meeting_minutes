# Meeting Summarizer — macOS MVP

## Scope

The first complete product is macOS-first. It focuses on a reliable local meeting record, from capture through review. iOS and iPadOS remain a later companion decision.

The MVP includes:

1. Create or import a local meeting recording.
2. Record permitted macOS microphone and selected-screen content through OS-level services.
3. Create a local timestamped transcript.
4. Perform speaker diarization with rename, merge, and split controls.
5. Present a timeline that synchronizes recording, transcript, speaker labels, and extracted visual attachments.
6. Create local structured minutes: summary, decisions, action items, and open questions.
7. Link transcript and minutes directly to relevant timestamps and attachments.
8. Provide optional, configurable local alerts when a monitored name or subject appears in the live transcript; each alert links to the source moment.
9. Support local search, export, and permanent deletion.

## Explicitly deferred

Calendar bots, third-party integrations, automatic sharing, organization-wide administration, persistent facial identification, and automatic cross-device synchronization are not part of the MVP.

## Readiness for internal use

The MVP is ready for team use when it can process representative meetings entirely offline, preserve source traceability, make speaker correction quick, and produce minutes that the team can review and trust.
