# Sprint 1 — Feedback

## Chair-assisted participant identification

Status: Promoted to PBI-017 (Proposed)

In a later increment, the local operator acting as meeting chair may explicitly
state the participant list. The system could also suggest participants from
locally supplied information, such as an email message selected or imported by
the operator. This is not MVP scope and must preserve the local-only boundary:
it does not authorize mailbox access, account integration, or external lookup.

Promotion: The Product Owner promoted this proposal to the root Product Backlog
as PBI-017 with `Status: Proposed` on 2026-10-01. It remains unscheduled.
