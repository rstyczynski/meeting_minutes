Sprint 3 PBI-012 analyzes these measurements before selecting architecture changes. Reliable affected-person identification, an operator comparison of alternate input, natural minutes accuracy, bounded review-player replay, portable MLX packaging, and representative Polish meeting accuracy remain open limitations. No production-quality claim is made for them.
