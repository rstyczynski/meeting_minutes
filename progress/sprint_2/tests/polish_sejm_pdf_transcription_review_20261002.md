# Sejm transcription checked against the official sitting record

The reference is the [Sejm full sitting record](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/sejm/term10/committees/GOR/sittings/39/pdf), downloaded as `/private/tmp/meeting-polish-gor-20241016.pdf` (SHA-256 `a2720b1aff7d8cc0be596e5f23ba7e8a0b3675a706b2626cce14cf06d1b89a44`). The hypothesis is the saved Parakeet v3 record `CA95C7AB-695E-4501-9176-938F0D463DFC` in `/private/tmp/meeting-polish-gor-sprint2`, generated from the [10-minute Sejm WAV](../polish_sejm_meeting_fixture.md). Its media SHA-256 is `1045d5199ea673b56c63f77c02a59b9a6be7c2887f1922253c887e2f93f184b9`. The PDF text was extracted with `pypdf` 6.10.0 and manually compared with the saved time-stamped words on 2026-10-02.

The PDF is a polished full-sitting record, without audio timestamps. The ASR record begins at about 108 seconds with room/time-limit remarks that are absent from the PDF; formal proceedings begin in the ASR at about 181 seconds. The ten-minute WAV ends while Ewa Zielińska is beginning her presentation. Therefore this review aligns the formal-proceedings span around 181–600 seconds to PDF pages 4–5. It excludes PDF material after the excerpt and audio remarks omitted from the edited record. It is a source-content review, not a defensible whole-clip word error rate.

At about 181–222 seconds, the chair's opening, quorum declaration, 2025 budget-bill agenda, previous-protocol acceptance, and invitation to Ignacy Niemczycki all appear in both the PDF and ASR. The ASR loses or distorts some words: `Witam` becomes `Witwa`, `Gospodarki i Rozwoju` loses `i`, and `Panie ministrze` becomes `Panie Mistrze`. The sequence and purpose of the turn remain recognizable.

At about 225–520 seconds, the minister's budget presentation broadly follows PDF page 4 and the top of page 5. Spot-checked monetary facts are present in spoken-number form: revenue 50,857 thousand złoty (ASR `50 milionów 857 tysięcy`), EU project funds over 87,000 thousand (`ponad 87 mln`), and POLSA support 52,208 thousand (`52 miliony 208 tysięcy`). Other numeric phrases are malformed or ambiguous. For example, the PDF's 34,662,000 thousand złoty for PFR bond redemption corresponds to the ASR's `34 miliardy złote 662 mili`, whose trailing unit is truncated; the 35,867,000 thousand expenditure figure appears as `35 867 mlnów`. Organization names also degrade: `POLSA` becomes `Polsca`, and `CEIDG` becomes `CIDG`. These examples establish topic and several amount matches, but do not establish reliable verbatim or numeric transcription.

At about 521–566 seconds, both sources show the chair opening discussion, hearing no objection, giving a positive opinion on budget part 20 and agenda points 2–8, then moving to part 65. The ASR preserves the substantive decision, but renders the agenda point range as `28`, which is ambiguous without the PDF. At about 566–600 seconds, the ASR captures the handoff to Ewa Zielińska and the beginning of her PKN description, but runs her first and last name together as `Ewęzińską` and alters some surrounding words.

**Assessment:** The transcript is useful for locating the main turns and the positive-opinion decision in this excerpt. It contains visible word, name, acronym, and numerical-unit errors that require correction before using it as an authoritative meeting record. The PDF lets us make this evidence-based assessment; the prior statement that Sejm transcription quality was wholly unvalidated was too broad. A whole-excerpt WER or diarization accuracy claim still needs a manually aligned verbatim audio reference, because the edited PDF omits spoken remarks, changes surface wording and number formatting, and is not time aligned.
