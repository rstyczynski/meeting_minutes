# Product Backlog

### PBI-001. Establish the product vision

Produce `README.md` as the accepted product vision, using the Product Owner’s stated intent. It must clearly state the intended user value, problem, outcome, and important constraints.

Test: Review confirms that the product vision is understandable, internally consistent, and accepted by the Product Owner.

### PBI-002. Derive the initial project plan from the vision

Create the project-specific roadmap and propose the next sprint from the accepted vision. The resulting work must be prioritized, outcome-oriented, and open to iterative refinement.

Test: Review confirms that the roadmap and proposed next sprint are traceable to the accepted vision and ready for Product Owner acceptance.

### PBI-003. Define product use cases and requirements

Define the stakeholders, representative use cases, and functional and non-functional requirements needed to achieve the accepted vision.

Test: Review confirms that requirements are understandable, testable, and traceable to the vision and use cases.

### PBI-004. Establish the Product Backlog and MVP scope

Create and prioritize Product Owner-level product increments, then define the smallest valuable initial release scope and its acceptance criteria.

Test: Review confirms that the backlog is outcome-oriented and the MVP scope has measurable acceptance criteria and explicit exclusions.

### PBI-005. Identify constraints and risks

Identify material product, legal, technical, delivery, and usability constraints and risks affecting the initial release.

Test: Review confirms that significant risks have a planned mitigation or validation approach.

### PBI-006. Establish the candidate architecture

Define a candidate architecture that supports the MVP requirements and identifies assumptions requiring validation.

Test: Review confirms that the candidate architecture addresses essential requirements and significant risks.

### PBI-007. Review the Inception baseline

Review the vision, requirements, backlog, MVP scope, risks, and candidate architecture as one coherent Inception baseline.

Test: Review confirms that the baseline is internally consistent and ready for Elaboration.
