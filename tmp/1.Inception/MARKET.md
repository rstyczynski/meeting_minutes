# Meeting Summarizer — Market Products

## Purpose

This Inception deliverable tests the vision against comparable products. It identifies established expectations, validates where the proposed product is distinct, and surfaces opportunities to innovate rather than features to copy. It does not define commercial positioning, pricing, technical architecture, or library selection.

## Market products

**Cloud meeting assistants.** Otter, Fireflies, Fathom, Grain, and Avoma commonly provide recording or meeting bots, transcripts, speaker labels, summaries, action items, and integrations. They establish the expected user experience, but they generally do not meet the product’s local-only requirement.

**Meeting platforms.** Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet provide recording and, in some plans, native transcription or summaries. Their recordings can be useful import sources, but the product must not rely on their APIs or cloud services.

**Local transcription products.** MacWhisper, Aiko, and Whisper Transcription demonstrate private, on-device speech-to-text, most often for imported audio. The intended product expands this into a complete, reviewable meeting record with speaker attribution, visual context, minutes, and evidence links.

**Recording products.** Apple Voice Memos, QuickTime, and built-in screen recording solve media capture. Meeting Summarizer adds the local processing, timeline, and review layer.

## Vision-validation implication

The vision is not simply “AI meeting notes.” Its proposed differentiation is a local, evidence-first record: recording, attributable transcript, visual attachments, and direct links from any transcript or summary statement back to its source timestamp. The review should look for gaps and unmet needs that make this vision stronger, rather than treating existing features as a roadmap to copy.
